# Getting Started with RealEye.io

This guide walks you through setting up and running your first eye-tracking study on [RealEye.io](https://www.realeye.io).

---

## 1. Create an Account

Go to [https://www.realeye.io](https://www.realeye.io) and sign up for a free account. No credit card is required for the free tier.

---

## 2. Create a New Study

1. Log in to your dashboard
2. Click **New Study**
3. Give your study a name and optional description
4. Choose a **stimulus type**:
   - **Image** – Upload a PNG, JPG, or GIF
   - **Website** – Enter a URL to test a live web page
   - **Video** – Upload an MP4 video file
   - **5-Second Test** – Show an image briefly, then ask questions

---

## 3. Add Stimuli

Upload or link your stimuli. You can add multiple stimuli to a single study — participants will view them in order (or randomly, if you enable randomisation).

**Tips for good stimuli:**
- Use high-resolution images (at least 1280×800 px) for best results
- For website tests, ensure the URL is publicly accessible
- Keep video files under the size limit for your plan

---

## 4. Configure Study Settings

- **Exposure duration** – Set how long each stimulus is shown (or allow free viewing)
- **Questions** – Add pre- or post-stimulus survey questions
- **Randomisation** – Randomise stimulus order to reduce order effects
- **Participant limit** – Set the maximum number of responses to collect

---

## 5. Preview Your Study

Use the **Preview** button to walk through your study as a participant. This lets you check that stimuli load correctly and questions make sense before launching.

---

## 6. Launch and Share

Once you are happy with the study:

1. Click **Launch Study**
2. Copy the **study link**
3. Share it with your participants via email, social media, or a research panel

Participants will be guided through webcam calibration automatically before the study begins.

---

## 7. Monitor Responses

Track incoming responses in real time from your study dashboard. You can see the number of completed responses, drop-off rates, and quality indicators.

---

## 8. Analyse Results

Once you have collected enough responses:

- View **heatmaps** to see where participants looked most
- Watch **gaze replays** to see individual participant sessions
- Set up **Areas of Interest (AOIs)** to get fixation metrics for specific regions
- **Export raw data** as CSV for analysis in R, Python, or Excel
