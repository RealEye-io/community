# RealEye.io Platform Roadmap

This roadmap gives an overview of what the RealEye.io team is working on, what's planned next, and what's being considered for the future.

> **Note:** This roadmap reflects our current intentions and is subject to change. Priorities may shift based on user feedback, technical constraints, and business needs. Dates are indicative only.

Have feedback on the roadmap? Share it in [GitHub Discussions – Ideas](../../discussions/categories/ideas).

---

## ✅ Recently Shipped

These features have been released and are available to users now.

- **AOI (Areas of Interest) analysis** — Define regions on stimuli and get fixation metrics per AOI
- **Video stimulus support** — Upload and test video content alongside images and websites
- **Panel recruitment integration** — Recruit participants directly through integrated research panels
- **CSV data export** — Download raw gaze data for offline analysis
- **5-Second Tests** — Time-limited stimulus exposure with post-exposure recall questions

---

## 🚧 In Progress

These items are currently being actively developed.

- **Improved calibration accuracy** — Refinements to the webcam calibration algorithm to improve gaze precision across a wider range of hardware and lighting conditions
- **Study templates** — Pre-built study configurations for common research scenarios (first-click tests, preference tests, etc.)
- **Enhanced reporting dashboard** — Richer in-app analytics with more filtering and comparison options

---

## 📋 Planned

These are items we intend to build next, roughly in priority order.

- **Mobile participant support** — Allow participants to join studies from smartphones and tablets
- **Collaborative workspaces** — Share studies and results with team members within a shared workspace
- **Longitudinal study support** — Run the same participants through multiple sessions over time
- **API access** — Programmatic access to study creation, data retrieval, and participant management
- **Integrations** — Native integrations with popular UX research tools (e.g. Figma, UserTesting, Maze)

---

## 💡 Under Consideration

These ideas are on our radar but not yet committed to. Your votes and comments in [Discussions](../../discussions/categories/ideas) help us prioritise.

- **Eye-tracking for accessibility testing** — Specific workflows for evaluating accessibility with gaze data
- **AI-powered insights** — Automated summaries and recommendations based on gaze patterns
- **Segment analysis** — Compare gaze behaviour across demographic groups or participant segments
- **White-label / custom branding** — Allow organisations to present studies under their own branding
- **Offline / kiosk mode** — Run studies in controlled lab environments without internet dependency

---

## 🙅 Not Planned

These are frequently requested items we have decided not to pursue at this time, with our reasoning.

- **Hardware eye-tracker integration** — RealEye.io is designed as a webcam-only solution. We do not plan to add support for dedicated eye-tracking hardware in the near term.
